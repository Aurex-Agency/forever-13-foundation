Who he was

He had a heart far bigger than his 13 years.

Connor was one of the kindest, most humble, and well-mannered children anyone could ever meet. He loved helping people and never expected anything in return. After we lost him in August 2024, our community carried us — and we started Forever 13 to carry that same love forward.

Connor's story

The boy who crossed the street just to say hello.

A perfect example of who Connor was is our neighbor, Miss Jones.

Every day after getting off the school bus, Connor would walk across the street just to sit and talk with her. On holidays, he made sure he had a plate of food and personally took it to her.

That's just who he was. He cared about people. He loved giving. He loved helping. His generous, caring spirit touched more lives than any of us realized in his time here.

Keep Smiling.

Connor's words — and the way we choose to remember him

In his memory, we keep his song close: “Dancing in the Sky.”

The promise we made

Turning pain into purpose, one family at a time.

When we lost Connor, a flood of people covered funeral costs, brought us food, and prayed us through the hardest days of our lives. Forever 13 exists to pass that same support forward — so no family in our community walks through tragedy alone.
