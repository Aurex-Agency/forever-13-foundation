Who he was

He had a heart far bigger than his 13 years.

Connor was one of the kindest, most humble, and well mannered children anyone could ever meet. He loved helping people, and he never expected a thing in return.

Miss Jones.

Every day after the school bus, Connor crossed the street just to sit and talk with our neighbor. On holidays, he fixed her a plate of food and carried it over himself. That was simply who he was.

Connor's story

The boy who loved to give.

He cared about people. He loved giving. He loved helping. That was Connor, all the way through.

After we lost him in August 2024, our community wrapped around us. People covered funeral costs, brought food to the door, and prayed us through the hardest days of our lives. His caring spirit had reached further than any of us ever knew.

Forever 13 is how we say thank you. We give that same love back to the next family who needs it, in his name.
