Our Mission

We give back the way our community gave to us.

Turning pain into purpose — one family at a time.

Our family started the Forever 13 Foundation in honor and memory of Connor Taylor.

After Connor passed unexpectedly in August 2024, a flood of people came to help. They covered funeral costs, brought us food, prayed for us, and carried us through the hardest days of our lives. His generous, caring spirit had touched more people than we ever realized.

Forever 13 exists to pass that same support forward. We help families facing sudden, unexpected hardship, through community support, fundraising, and simple acts of kindness, so that no one walks through tragedy alone.

Connor's Story

He had a heart far bigger than his 13 years.

Connor was one of the kindest, most humble, and well-mannered children anyone could ever meet. He loved helping people and never expected anything in return.

A perfect example of who he was is our neighbor, Miss Jones. Every day after getting off the school bus, Connor would walk across the street just to sit and talk with her. On holidays, he made sure he had a plate of food and personally took it to her.

That's just who he was. He cared about people. He loved giving. He loved helping.

“Keep Smiling.”
Connor's words, the way we choose to remember him

In his memory, we keep his song close: “Dancing in the Sky.”
