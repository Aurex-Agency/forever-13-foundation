The heart behind it all

Meet Connor.

Forever 13 exists because of one remarkable kid. This is the boy whose kindness started everything.

Connor Taylor had a heart far bigger than his 13 years. He cared about people. He loved giving. He loved helping.

He was one of the kindest, most humble, and well mannered children anyone could ever meet, and he never expected a thing in return.

Miss Jones.

Every day after the school bus, Connor crossed the street just to sit and talk with our neighbor. On holidays, he fixed her a plate of food and carried it over himself. That was simply who he was.

After we lost him in August 2024, we learned his caring spirit had reached further than any of us ever knew. Forever 13 is how we say thank you, by giving that same love to the next family who needs it.
