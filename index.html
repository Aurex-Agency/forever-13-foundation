04

The heart behind it all

Meet Connor.

Connor Taylor had a heart far bigger than his 13 years. He cared about people. He loved giving. He loved helping.

He was one of the kindest, most humble, and well mannered kids anyone could ever meet, and he never expected a thing in return.

Miss Jones.

Every day after the school bus, Connor crossed the street just to sit and talk with our neighbor. On holidays, he fixed her a plate of food and carried it over himself. That was simply who he was.

We lost Connor in August 2024. In the days after, we learned his caring spirit had reached further than any of us ever knew. Forever 13 is how we say thank you: by giving that same love to the next family who needs it.

06

Good to know

Questions families ask us.

How can I donate?

Tap any Donate button on this site to give through CashApp or Venmo, or scan our QR code at any Forever 13 event. Every gift, of any size, helps a local family.

Where does my donation go?

Straight to families in our community facing sudden, unexpected hardship. Funds are managed with absolute care by our treasurer and the family board.

Who runs Forever 13?

Connor's own family. President Collin Walters leads the board, and Connor's mother Brandy handles outreach. Nobody here is a stranger to why this work matters.

How did the foundation begin?

We lost Connor in August 2024, when he was 13. Our community covered costs, brought meals, and carried us through. Forever 13 was founded to pass that same support forward.

Why the name "Forever 13"?

Connor was 13, and to us he always will be. The angel wings in our logo and the words he lived by, keep smiling, are the spirit of everything we do.

Can I host a fundraiser or event?

Absolutely. Reach out through our email or socials and we will help you plan it, promote it, and make it count for a family in need.
