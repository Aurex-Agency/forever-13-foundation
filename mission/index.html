Our Mission

Turning pain into purpose, one family at a time.

The Forever 13 Foundation supports families facing unexpected hardships through community support, fundraising, and acts of kindness, in loving memory of Connor Taylor.

When the worst day comes, nobody around here faces it alone.

A warm meal on the porch. A room full of people who refuse to let you fall. That is what our community gave us when we lost Connor, and it is exactly what we give back, family by family.

When we lost Connor, our community covered funeral costs, brought food to the door, and prayed for us through the hardest days of our lives. The Forever 13 Foundation exists to pass that same support forward.
